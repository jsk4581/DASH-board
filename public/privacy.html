DASH Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

DASH (Daily Agenda & Schedule Hub) is a personal schedule board and notebook. It has no accounts and no server of its own.

What the app stores

Your boards, tasks, due dates and memos are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere unless you choose to export it (as a JSON file through the share sheet) or, in the web version, connect your own GitHub Gist for sync.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal data, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or location. It makes no network requests on its own.

Optional GitHub Gist sync (web version only)

If you connect a GitHub token in the web version, your board is written to a secret Gist in your own GitHub account and the token is kept only in that browser's local storage. That data is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. The Android app does not include this feature.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings) removes everything it stored. Exported files are yours to keep or delete.
